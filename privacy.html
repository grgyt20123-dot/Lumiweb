Privacy Policy

A clear overview of how LUMI may handle information while helping Discord communities.

Last updated: November 27, 2026

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how LUMI (“the Bot”) handles information when it is used in Discord servers. LUMI is an independent Discord bot and is not operated by Discord. By using the Bot, you acknowledge this policy.

2. Information the Bot may process

Depending on which features a server administrator enables or members use, LUMI may process:

  • Discord identifiers: user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs and message IDs needed to carry out commands and server features.
  • Server configuration: settings selected by administrators, such as configured channels, roles, moderation options, welcome settings and ticket settings.
  • Feature activity: command names, feature settings and moderation or ticket records when those features are used or enabled.
  • Messages and submitted content: content supplied in commands, forms or support tickets, where required to provide that feature.

The Bot is not intended to collect passwords, payment-card details or government identity documents. Do not submit sensitive personal information through Bot commands or tickets.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to operate commands, apply server configurations, provide moderation and logging features, manage tickets, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors and maintain the Bot. LUMI does not use server content to sell advertising profiles.

4. Storage and retention

Some settings and feature records may be stored so they continue to work after the Bot restarts. Information is kept only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the relevant feature, operational purposes or legitimate record-keeping. Exact retention can vary by feature and server configuration.

Server administrators should avoid enabling logging features for information they do not need. Where available, administrators can remove configured features or records through the Bot's controls.

5. Sharing and third parties

LUMI does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Discord as part of Discord's platform and infrastructure. Hosting or infrastructure providers may also process technical data needed to run the Bot. Information may be disclosed if required by applicable law or to address abuse, security or safety issues.

6. Your choices and data requests

Server administrators control whether to invite LUMI, which permissions to grant and which optional features to enable. You can stop interacting with the Bot, and a server administrator can remove it from a server.

For a privacy or deletion request, contact the LUMI team through the LUMI support server. Include the relevant server or user ID and describe the data or feature involved. Do not post private credentials or sensitive documents. We may need to verify a request before acting on it and may retain limited information where legally or operationally necessary.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information. However, no online service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users and server administrators should keep their Discord accounts secure and grant the Bot only the permissions needed.

8. Children and age requirements

LUMI is provided for use on Discord in accordance with Discord's minimum age requirements and applicable local law. The Bot is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of those requirements.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as LUMI's features or legal requirements change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated date. Continued use after an update means the updated policy applies to future use.

10. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or data requests, contact the LUMI team through the support server.
